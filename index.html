生效日：2026 年 9 月 19 日　·　版本 1.0

English version below ↓

摘要

  • 本遊戲沒有帳號系統，我們不會為您建立個人檔案。
  • 對局紀錄、設定與匯出的圖片只存放在您的裝置上。
  • 單機模式完全不連網。
  • 使用聯機對局時，配對資訊會短暫經過我們的伺服器（十分鐘後刪除）， 且第三方 STUN 服務會取得您的 IP 位址。
  • 本遊戲顯示 Google AdMob 廣告。廣告不影響對局結果。

本摘要僅供快速理解，完整內容以下方條款為準。

一、資料控制者

本政策適用於行動遊戲《落墨成勢》（Ink Builds Worlds，以下稱「本遊戲」）之 Android 與 iOS 版本。

控制者FireCat Studio（個人開發者）
聯絡方式carter870808@gmail.com

本遊戲未指定資料保護長（DPO），亦未達法規要求指定之門檻。

二、我們處理的資料

2.1　儲存於您裝置上的資料

下列資料僅寫入本機儲存空間，不會傳輸至我們或任何第三方：

類別內容
對局與練習紀錄日期時間、遊戲模式、比分、重疊率、該局盤面縮圖
應用程式設定顯示名稱、音量、語言、節能模式、輔助提示開關
匯出圖片由您主動選擇「存到相簿」時產生之 PNG 檔

解除安裝本遊戲即會刪除上述資料。已由您存入系統相簿之圖片不在此限， 其管理權歸屬於您的裝置。

2.2　聯機對局

下列處理僅於您主動使用「聯機雙人」功能時發生。

（a）顯示名稱
您於設定頁輸入之名稱將傳送予對手並顯示於其畫面。未填寫時顯示為預設名稱。 本遊戲不驗證此名稱，亦不將其與任何身分資訊連結。 請避免填入真實姓名或其他您不願為不特定人知悉之資訊。

（b）配對中繼
為使兩台裝置建立直接連線，下列資訊會暫存於我們自行部署之 Cloudflare Workers 服務：房間代碼、WebRTC 連線描述（其中包含網路位址候選）、顯示名稱。

用途僅用於協助兩位玩家建立連線
保存期間十分鐘，或於連線建立後即刻刪除，以較早者為準
後續利用無。不進行分析、剖析或任何形式之再利用

連線建立後，對局資料由兩台裝置直接往來，不經過我們的伺服器。

（c）STUN 服務
建立直接連線須確認雙方在網際網路上的位址，此步驟使用下列第三方 STUN 服務， 該等服務將取得您的 IP 位址：

  • Cloudflare, Inc.（stun.cloudflare.com）
  • Google LLC（stun.l.google.com）

2.3　廣告

本遊戲透過 Google AdMob 顯示廣告，用以支應開發與維運成本。廣告出現於三個時點： 應用程式啟動時一次、數場對局之間一次、以及您主動點選「贊助作者」時。

Google 可能蒐集並使用廣告識別碼及相關資訊，以提供、個人化及衡量廣告成效。 其處理方式請參閱 Google 合作夥伴網站與應用程式政策。

廣告不影響對局結果。本遊戲之勝負判定為確定性演算， 觀看廣告不會取得任何步數、道具或對局優勢；「贊助作者」之回饋僅為一枚紀念性落款印記。

2.4　我們不處理的資料

  • 不蒐集姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼、通訊錄或精確位置資訊
  • 不使用分析或使用者追蹤 SDK（第 2.3 節所述廣告所必需者除外）
  • 不販售個人資料，亦不為第三方之行為廣告目的分享個人資料
  • 不於伺服器端保存您的對局內容
  • 不進行對您產生法律效果或類似重大影響之自動化決策或剖析

Privacy Policy Ink Builds Worlds（《落墨成勢》）

Effective 19 September 2026　·　Version 1.0

Summary

  • The game has no accounts. We do not build a profile of you.
  • Match records, settings and exported images are stored only on your device.
  • Single-player modes make no network connections at all.
  • Online play briefly passes matchmaking data through our server (deleted after ten minutes), and third-party STUN services receive your IP address.
  • The game shows Google AdMob advertising. Ads have no effect on play.

This summary is for orientation only; the sections below govern.

This policy applies to the Android and iOS versions of the mobile game Ink Builds Worlds (《落墨成勢》, “the game”).

2. Data we process

2.1　Data stored on your device

The following is written to local storage only. It is not transmitted to us or to any third party:

CategoryContents
Match and practice recordsDate and time, game mode, scores, overlap ratio, a thumbnail of the board
Application settingsDisplay name, volume, language, power-saving mode, assistance toggles
Exported imagesPNG files created when you choose to save a board to your photo library

Uninstalling the game deletes this data. Images you have already saved to your system photo library are not affected and remain under your control.

2.2　Online play

The following occurs only when you actively use the online multiplayer feature.

(a) Display name
The name you enter in settings is sent to your opponent and shown on their screen. If left blank, a default name is shown. We do not verify this name or link it to any identity. Please avoid entering your real name or anything you would not wish an unknown person to see.

(b) Matchmaking relay
To allow two devices to connect directly, the following is temporarily stored on a Cloudflare Workers service operated by us: the room code, the WebRTC session description (which includes network address candidates), and the display name.

PurposeSolely to connect two players
RetentionTen minutes, or immediately upon the connection being established, whichever is earlier
Further useNone. No analysis, profiling or any secondary use

Once connected, match data passes directly between the two devices and does not traverse our servers.

(c) STUN services
Establishing a direct connection requires determining each side's address on the internet. This step uses the following third-party STUN services, which receive your IP address:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (stun.cloudflare.com)
  • Google LLC (stun.l.google.com)

2.3　Advertising

The game displays advertising through Google AdMob to cover the cost of development and operation. Ads appear at three points: once when the application is opened, once between every few matches, and when you choose “Support the Author”.

Google may collect and use an advertising identifier and related information to serve, personalise and measure advertising. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Advertising has no effect on play. The game's outcome is determined by a deterministic simulation; watching an advertisement grants no moves, items or advantage of any kind. The reward for “Support the Author” is a commemorative seal only.

2.4　What we do not process

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, contacts or precise location
  • We use no analytics or user-tracking SDK, other than what the advertising in §2.3 requires
  • We do not sell personal data, nor share it for third-party behavioural advertising
  • We do not retain your match content on any server
  • We carry out no automated decision-making or profiling producing legal or similarly significant effects
